> selected_research

📡 Cross-Modal Knowledge Distillation for Sensor-less Agitation Detection

Developing a healthcare AI pipeline that transfers information from wearable physiological signals to ambient Wi-Fi CSI representations for privacy-preserving agitation detection in Alzheimer's care.

Core themes: cross-modal learning • Wi-Fi sensing • wearable physiology • healthcare AI • leakage-aware evaluation • privacy-preserving monitoring

***
🧬 Electrophysiology + Spiking Neural Networks

Building and evaluating spiking neural networks (SNNs) and recurrent spiking architectures for temporally structured electrophysiological and biomedical signal classification.

Core themes: neuromorphic AI • temporal learning • biosignals • SNNs • biomedical intelligence

***

Contributed to a $35,000 industry-funded healthcare AI collaboration with InComm Payments focused on claims-validated reward optimization and ROI maximization in Medicare Advantage.
